More detail — the numbers, and how to keep it

Why listing everything is worth the trouble. One picture that names four things gives four pieces of teaching instead of one, and a picture gets reused by everything in it — so the same twenty photographs go a great deal further. It is also how real image collections are labelled. Underneath, that means the network answers a separate yes/no for each thing it knows rather than picking a single winner, which is why it can answer with more than one thing at a time.
